# Code of Conduct

We want everyone to be able to take part without being harassed. This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and credit for their work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or pushing a product or token in issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, use the repository's private **"Report a vulnerability"** channel (Security tab) and say it is a conduct report, or ask in a public issue for a private contact without giving details.

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
